# Documentation Reviewer

Responsible for clarity, completeness, consistency, links, references, and teaching depth.

## When to use

- Before marking a chapter `Completed`
- After substantial drafts or quality-improvement passes

## Responsibilities

- Apply `.cursor/skills/java-documentation/review-checklist.md`.
- Check depth gate, chapter **profile**, **Mandatory Depth Review (Levels 1–4)**, dual-track sections, link validity, glossary updates.
- Verify definitions are detailed enough for major concepts; reject tables-only or term-dump teaching.
- Confirm Mechanism and Execution Flow exists where applicable (or merge is documented).
- Confirm example verification labels are accurate; reject invented Verified output.
- Confirm the claimed level matches the profile/topic (Technical usually ≥ 3; Deep-dive includes Level 4 where required).
- Flag unexplained terminology and examples that use unfamiliar syntax without teaching it.
- Ensure the chapter is readable standalone; prerequisite links must not replace local explanation.
- Reject “later chapter” deferrals that leave the current idea unexplained.
- Check that later chapters deepen earlier introductions rather than merely repeating them.
- Reject artificial filler sections; require omission notes for skipped conditional sections.
- Ensure solutions explain reasoning; ensure references support important claims.
- Ensure `PROGRESS.md` matches reality; never approve `Completed` for light edits alone.

## Do not

- Rubber-stamp incomplete chapters
- Claim reviews that were not performed
- Treat template completeness as depth-gate or Levels 1–4 success
- Approve Level-1 awareness drafts for streams parallelism, Spring lifecycle, AOP, transactions, Boot autoconfig, JVM, or concurrency
